# Deviations: defects in the original programs and how the corrected model treats them

The faithful ports reproduce the original research programs exactly, including everything below. The corrected model ([model.md](model.md)) was written fresh from the physics, not by patching the originals, so most items are resolved by its formulation rather than by a one-line fix. Each entry gives what the original did, its effect where measured, and the corrected model's treatment.

"Charge line" is the original `ZnMn02_v3` (N = 5, used for parameter sweeps); "pH-cell line" is `ZnMn02_v2.1_GITT_no_probe` (N = 6, H⁺ transported).

| # | Where | What the original did | Effect | Corrected model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-1 | charge line, initialization | `z_ion(4) = z_Mn0`: an undeclared variable (implicitly REAL, zero), so **Mn²⁺ has charge 0**: no migration, and the initial SO₄²⁻ from electroneutrality omits Mn²⁺. | Measured on representative runs (only this line corrected): up to 3–8 mV in voltage, up to 1.6 % in capacity; small because Mn²⁺ (0.05 M) is a small part of the cations in 2 M ZnSO₄. | Charges are fixed constants (2, 2, −2, +1). |
| M-2 | main loop | Capacity on charge: `mAhg − c_specific·Δt` with a negative `c_specific`, so charge capacity adds. | Wrong capacity sign on charge. | Capacity is the integral of I dt. |
| M-3 | main loop | Exits: lower cutoff on Φ₁ − Φ₂ at the collector, upper cutoff on Φ₁ alone, "end of charge" on Φ₂ ≥ 99 (a placeholder). | Inconsistent cutoffs; charge never ends physically. | Cutoffs on the cell voltage (collector vs the Zn anode); a discharge ends at V_min, a charge at V_max; physical limits are reported ([protocol.md](protocol.md)). |
| M-4 | R2 | The reaction area switches with the sign of the current (ZHS area on charge, ZMO area on discharge). | Discontinuous kinetics at every current reversal. | One rate law, smooth in the potential; growth on foreign surfaces only while deposition is favoured, zero at equilibrium. With `zhs = lumped`, deposition on the ZHS area (the original's intent, made smooth). |
| M-5 | R1–R5 | Rates clipped after evaluation (boundary-layer limits, "solid consumption limits") inside the finite-difference Jacobian; in two R2 branches the H⁺ rate is not rescaled with the others. | Non-smooth Jacobian; stoichiometry inconsistent, so mass is not conserved (M-15). | No clipping. Solids are unknowns of the implicit system; areas vanish with the amounts, so nothing goes negative. |
| M-6 | R3 | The Zn-limiting branch rescales with c_SO₄ instead of c_Zn. | Wrong limiting rate. | No limiting branches (M-5). |
| M-7 | Jacobian | Finite-difference steps relative to the value (zero at zero concentration); the Φ₂ step uses Φ₁ (charge line). | Inaccurate Jacobian. | Analytic Jacobian for storage, electroneutrality and transport; finite differences only for the local sources, with safe relative steps. |
| M-8 | R2 | The equilibrium potential and the exchange current imply different stoichiometries. | Kinetics inconsistent with the stated reaction. | One reaction, Zn_zMnO₂ + 4H⁺ + (2−2z)e⁻ ⇌ zZn²⁺ + Mn²⁺ + 2H₂O, used for both. |
| M-9 | solids | Solids updated explicitly after each solve, from a midpoint state; inserted material moved to ZMO when z ≥ 0.985·z_max. | Operator-splitting error; contributes to M-15. | Solids implicit in the same Newton system (exact conservation). The 0.985·z_max transfer is not modelled (an improvement item). |
| M-10 | pH module | ZHS solubility exponent +1/6 in one line and −1/6 in the other; K_sp defaults and units differ between files. | The pH depends on which file ran (pH-cell line: about −4.4 in the cathode with K_sp = 7×10⁻²⁶). | One ZHS equilibrium, Q = c_Zn⁴ c_SO₄ / c_H⁶ against 10^logK_ZHS (default 28.4, Herrmann et al.). |
| M-11 | pH module | The pH spline is evaluated in single precision. | Noise of about 10⁻⁶ pH. | `ph_mode = spline` evaluates it in double precision; the default computes the pH from the equilibria. |
| M-12 | throughout | Implicit typing and single-precision literals (e.g. `Rigc = 8.314`, `Fconst = 96485`, many constants), which is how M-1 went unnoticed. | Constants rounded to single precision. | Double precision throughout; CODATA constants. |
| M-14 | charge line, output | `pH_standard` reads `cprev(6, j)` with N = 5, i.e. Φ₁ of the next node. | Output column meaningless (about −3.2); no feedback. | Not applicable. |
| M-15 | both lines | The programs' own conservation check: S falls to 80–91 % of its initial amount over a discharge (charge line); S rises 2 % and Mn falls 15 % (pH-cell line). | Results drift. | Zn, Mn, S and H conserved to round-off, tested over several cycles. |
| M-16 | update loop | A leftover loop index writes pH values to the wrong node. | Output only. | Not applicable. |
| M-17 | R3 OCP | The spline lookup reads outside its table for θ outside [0, 1]. | Undefined values (the faithful port stops if it happens with a finite θ). | θ is in (0, 1) by construction (log-odds unknown). |
| M-18 | pH-cell R5 | The ZHS "top-up" compares H⁺ **moles** in a control volume (itself mixing mol and mol/cm³) with a **concentration** threshold, and adds the difference as a rate. | ZHS grows without bound (mean 0.66 g/cm³ by the end of a run); H⁺ floors at 10⁻²⁰. | Finite-rate precipitation driven by the supersaturation. |
| M-19 | pH-cell line | Transport coefficients recomputed after the porosity update only for interior cathode nodes; the boundary nodes keep their initial values. | Inconsistent coefficients at the separator interface and the collector. | Coefficients computed from the current porosity at every face. |
| M-20 | pH-cell line | `PROBE_NODE` undeclared in the initialization (its declaration is commented out): implicitly double and uninitialized. | Harmless without a reservoir; wrong with one. | The probe region is a mesh region with its own porosity and tortuosity. |
| M-21 | pH-cell runs | Only R5 is switched on; the time step is forced to 0.1 s between 89 and 100 mAh/g (a patch). | — | All reactions available as options; adaptive sub-steps. |

**Not ported: the probe line.** A third original program, `ZnMn02_v2.1_GITT_pHcell_updated`, adds a pH-probe reservoir region (with Δt = 0.01 s and adaptive finite-difference steps). No archived output is known to have been produced by that exact source, so a faithful port could not be checked against it, and it is not provided. Its physics, an open electrolyte region between the anode and the separator, is the corrected model's probe region (`L_probe`, `eps_probe`, `tau_probe`).

**Reservoir.** Both lines represent the electrolyte reservoir by stretching the separator and multiplying its transport coefficients by the stretch factor squared. The corrected model has a probe region of open electrolyte (porosity 1) of a given length instead.

**Charge.** The original charge model never fully worked. In the corrected model a discharge followed by a charge recovers 99 % of the capacity. Two things were needed: a consistent formulation (implicit solids, rate laws that vanish at equilibrium, the pH from the solution's equilibria) and an insertion OCP with Nernstian ends ([options.md](options.md)).
